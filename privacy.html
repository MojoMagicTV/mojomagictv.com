Privacy

This site keeps simple visit stats so MojoMagicTV can see how the page is used and make it better. Here is everything that is collected.

What is collected

  • Which pages you open and the order you open them in.
  • How long the page is open and visible.
  • Which parts of the page come into view, and which links or buttons are clicked (their label only, never anything typed).
  • Browser, operating system and device type (phone, tablet or computer), a rough screen size, and your browser language.
  • The name of the site that linked you here, if any.
  • Your approximate country.

What is not collected

  • No IP addresses are saved. Your country is looked up when the visit is received, and your IP address is used only briefly to block abuse and is never saved.
  • No cookies, no advertising or cross-site tracking, no fingerprinting, and no screen or session recordings.
  • Each visit gets a random ID kept in your browser tab only. It is deleted when you close the tab.

Who sees it

Only the site owner. The stats are never sold or shared. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the stats are received and stored by Cloudflare, which act only as hosting providers. Stats are kept for up to 13 months, then deleted.

Opting out

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, no stats are collected. Blocking scripts also works.

Questions

Contact the site owner through MojoMagicTV's official channels.
